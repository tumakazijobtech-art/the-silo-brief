The Sh3.3 trillion gap that no one can see.

Did you know

Only 16 in every 100 Kenyan MSMEs can access formal credit. The other 6.2 million are locked out by a collateral requirement they cannot meet.

What we are studying

Five instruments that could close the visibility gap

01

M-Pesa transaction histories

Converting mobile money records into portable credit signals.

Literature review done
02

Verifiable skills credentials

Digital badges for informal trades with no formal certification.

Scoping
03

WhatsApp record-keeping

Job and payment logs captured in the tool operators already use.

Prototype
04

Chama buying groups

Bulk purchasing built on existing savings associations.

Under review
05

Mutual guarantees

Group accountability as a substitute for individual collateral.
